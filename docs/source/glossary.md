# Glossary

The words these pages use, in braid theory and in braiding.

**Artin generator** — `σᵢ`, one crossing: the strand in place `i` over the
strand in place `i + 1`; `σᵢ⁻¹` under. See {doc}`usage`.

**bobbin** — what a yarn is wound on, and whose weight pulls it taut: a tama
on a marudai, a carrier's spool on a machine.

**braid word** — a braid written as its crossings in order: `σ₁σ₂⁻¹`, `1 -2`,
`aB`. See {doc}`braid_representations`.

**bridge** — a way back from a braid to how to make it: a kumihimo disk's
moves, a lace punchcard, a sinnet on Ashley's stand. See {doc}`bridges`.

**carrier** — what holds a yarn on a braiding machine, or a slot holding it
on a disk, and moves it.

**closure** — the knot or link a braid makes when each strand's end is joined
to its start.

**fell** — where the braid is being formed: the yarns above it are free, the
braid below it made.

**fingerprint** — the few numbers braidpy compares braids by, the same
however a braid is written. See {doc}`comparing_braids`.

**Grow** — Braid Studio's replay of a braid being made. See
[Braid Studio](braid_studio.md).

**horn gear (maypole) machine** — a braiding machine whose carriers ride
round a ring of turning gears, half one way, half the other.

**Jacquard lace machine** — a horn gear machine whose gears are switched by a
punched card, so that it braids a pattern.

**kumihimo** — Japanese braiding; here, mostly on a **disk** of slots
(**mobidai**, foam disk) or a **marudai**.

**laid** — a braid as it first comes off what made it, before it is
tightened.

**marudai** — the round Japanese braiding stand: yarns hang on bobbins over a
polished mirror, the braid forming below its hole. See {doc}`marudai`.

**permutation** — where a braid takes each strand: the strand starting in
place `i` ends in place `perm()[i - 1]`.

**pure braid** — a braid that brings every strand back to its place.

**ring program** — a braid as neighbours swapping places round a ring: the
common step between the ways of making it. See {doc}`bridges`.

**settled** — a braid tightened until its yarns press on each other: sideways,
on a marudai, or by physics. See {doc}`rope_physics`.

**sinnet** — a braid of several strands; **Ashley's solid sinnets** are those
of *The Ashley Book of Knots* worked on his stand, a disk with spaces.

**strand**, **yarn** — strand in the mathematics, yarn in the braid made: a
yarn has a thickness.
