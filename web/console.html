Working it

  • Click a slot to put a carrier on it, or to take one off — at any step.
  • A collision stops the run and is ringed in red. Take one of the two carriers off and carry on.
  • Stepping back restores what was there before, so you can undo a step you did not like.
  • Switched contacts are drawn as a double mark: there a carrier may be sent back the way it came.
