Braid representations

A braid can be written down many ways. braidpy reads and writes each of these, and moves between them.

A braid word

A braid on n strands is written as its crossings in order, each an Artin generator: σᵢ crosses the strand in place i over the one in place i + 1, σᵢ⁻¹ under (Getting started). braidpy keeps a braid as the list of signed indices, Braid([1, -2], n_strands=3) for σ₁σ₂⁻¹, and writes it in the usual notations:

In letters, a is σ₁, b is σ₂ and so on; a capital is the inverse.

A ring word, round a core

A tube’s strands go round its axis, and a braid word, its strands in a row, cannot say so: a tube and a flat braid curled up have the same word. A ring word (braidpy.annulus_braid, written as Braid Studio takes it, ring word) can: its strands are in a ring round the axis, crossing n crosses the two either side of a seam and n + 1 turns the ring a slot. Kept as a strand, the core tells the two apart: cored_word is the braid word with the core as strand 1, solid_word the one with the middle filled.

ring_word() reads a machine’s braid as a ring word round an axis: an axial, or any spot one could be fed at (axial_spots()), the core a machine braids round if not said (default_axis()). Round another axis the same machine makes another braid round its core. shortened() makes the word shorter, as shortened() does a braid word, the relations read round the ring:

from braidpy.horn_gear import examples
from braidpy.horn_gear.word import ring_word

word = ring_word(examples.tubular_braid_8())  # round the tube's hole
len(word.moves), len(word.shortened().moves)  # 40, 32: the turns cancel

In band generators

Round a circle — a kumihimo disk, a marudai — any two strands can cross directly, in front of those between: the band generator a_ts (Birman, Ko and Lee), σ_{t-1} ⋯ σ_{s+1} σ_s σ_{s+1}⁻¹ ⋯ σ_{t-1}⁻¹ in Artin’s letters, a_{s+1,s} being σ_s (braidpy.band_canonical_form). A ring word’s crossing across the seam is a_{n,1}, and its turn is δ = a_{n,n-1} ⋯ a_{2,1}. braid.band_word() writes a braid so, as its band normal form spells it, (t, s) for a_ts and (s, t) for its inverse; artin_from_band reads one back.

The crossings are each braid’s repeat, shortened. A braid of strands carried across the disk is shorter so; one of neighbours crossing, as most machines make, is longer.

Where the strands go

A braid takes the strand starting in each place to a place at the other end: its permutation. perm() gives where each strand ends, and permutations() the order of the strands after each crossing:

Braid([1, -2], n_strands=3).perm()  # [2, 3, 1]

A braid whose permutation is the identity is pure (braidpy.pure_braid). Many words share a permutation; the permutation forgets which strand passed over.

A matrix

The Burau representation sends each generator to a matrix in a variable t, and a braid to the product of its generators’ matrices (to_matrix(), to_reduced_matrix()). Equal braids have equal matrices; the reduced matrix gives the closure’s Alexander polynomial (braidpy.properties.alexander_polynomial()), an invariant of the knot or link the braid closes into.

A normal form

Of all the words making a braid, the Garside normal form picks one: a power of the half twist Δ, then a sequence of permutation braids, each as long as it can be (braidpy.garside_canonical_form, get_canonical_factors()). Two words make the same braid exactly when their normal forms are equal.

A shape in space

A braid word says nothing of a braid’s shape; braidpy lays one for it. to_parametric_strands() gives each strand as a curve, crossing over or under where the word says, for a 3D view (Visualizing braids). How a braid’s yarns settle into their real shape is the subject of Settling a braid by physics and Why a braid’s shape has no closed form.

A way of making it

A braid is also what a kumihimo disk’s moves, a braiding machine’s carriers, a lace machine’s punchcard or a sinnet on Ashley’s stand make. braidpy reads the braid word off each, and goes back from a braid to each (Making a braid by hand or on a machine).
