Glossary

The words these pages use, in braid theory and in braiding.

Artin generator — σᵢ, one crossing: the strand in place i over the strand in place i + 1; σᵢ⁻¹ under. See Getting started.

bobbin — what a yarn is wound on, and whose weight pulls it taut: a tama on a marudai, a carrier’s spool on a machine.

braid word — a braid written as its crossings in order: σ₁σ₂⁻¹, 1 -2, aB. See Braid representations.

way of making — how to make a given braid: a kumihimo disk’s moves, a lace punchcard, a sinnet on Ashley’s stand. See Making a braid by hand or on a machine.

carrier — what holds a yarn on a braiding machine, or a slot holding it on a disk, and moves it.

closure — the knot or link a braid makes when each strand’s end is joined to its start.

fell — where the braid is being formed: the yarns above it are free, the braid below it made.

fingerprint — the few numbers braidpy compares braids by, the same however a braid is written. See Comparing braids.

Grow — Braid Studio’s replay of a braid being made. See Braid Studio.

horn gear (maypole) machine — a braiding machine whose carriers ride round a ring of turning gears, half one way, half the other.

Jacquard lace machine — a horn gear machine whose gears are switched by a punched card, so that it braids a pattern.

kumihimo — Japanese braiding; here, mostly on a disk of slots (mobidai, foam disk) or a marudai.

laid — a braid as it first comes off what made it, before it is tightened.

marudai — the round Japanese braiding stand: yarns hang on bobbins over a polished mirror, the braid forming below its hole. See Making a braid on a marudai.

notch — on a lace machine, where two neighbouring gears overlap: a bobbin’s place between turns. The patents call it where two race-circles intersect. See the lace machine.

pattern bar — in the lace machine patents, the bar, set by the pattern, that clutches the quoits to be turned at a step: a line of braidpy’s punchcard.

permutation — where a braid takes each strand: the strand starting in place i ends in place perm()[i - 1].

pure braid — a braid that brings every strand back to its place.

quoit — in the lace machine patents, a gear’s carrier-engaging plate: braidpy’s gear of the lace ring. Each quoit is clutched to its drive and stops itself after a half turn.

race-circle — in the lace machine patents, the circle a carrier runs round a quoit; they intersect at the notches.

ring program — a braid as neighbours swapping places round a ring: the common step between the ways of making it. See Making a braid by hand or on a machine.

settled — a braid tightened until its yarns press on each other: sideways, on a marudai, or by physics. See Settling a braid by physics.

sinnet — a braid of several strands; Ashley’s solid sinnets are those of The Ashley Book of Knots worked on his stand, a disk with spaces.

strand, yarn — strand in the mathematics, yarn in the braid made: a yarn has a thickness.
